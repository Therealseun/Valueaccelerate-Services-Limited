Ask a Question
How long does CAC registration take?

Timelines depend on the Corporate Affairs Commission's processing at the time of your application, so we don't promise fixed dates. What we do is prepare your documentation correctly the first time to help avoid avoidable delays and rework — and we keep you informed at every stage.

What information do I need to register a business?

Typically you'll need your proposed business name (it's wise to have alternatives), the nature of your business, a valid means of identification, your contact details, and a business address. For company incorporation, details of directors and shareholders are also required. We'll give you a personalized checklist once we understand your needs.

What is the difference between a business name and a limited liability company?

A business name is generally simpler and suits many sole traders and small ventures, but the owner and the business are treated as one. A limited liability company is a separate legal entity, which can offer personal liability protection, greater credibility, and easier access to investment. The right choice depends on your goals — we'll help you weigh the options.

Can you help me register a company?

Yes. We support company incorporation end to end — name reservation, preparing incorporation documents, filing, and following through until your certificate and status report are ready.

Do you provide TIN/tax registration support?

Yes. We guide you through the relevant tax registrations for your business, including obtaining your Tax Identification Number and understanding the documentation involved.

Can you help an existing business with compliance?

Absolutely. We support existing businesses with compliance matters such as annual returns, status checks, and organizing corporate records so everything stays in good standing.

How do I begin the registration process?

It's simple: fill out the contact form below or message us on WhatsApp. We'll discuss what you need, outline the requirements and next steps, and get things moving from there.
